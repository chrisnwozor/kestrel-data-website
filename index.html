Case study

Canada Food Recall Data Warehouse

An end-to-end reporting system built on public Government of Canada food recall data, from raw files to a finished executive dashboard.

The challenge

Inconsistent spellings, missing fields and several reasons packed into a single column made basic counts unreliable.

The approach

Profiled every field, separated bad records for review instead of guessing, and modelled the data into a structured warehouse with a Power BI dashboard.

The outcome

Clear answers at a glance: nearly half of recalls with a known cause came from allergens, with milk the single most common reason.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What data do I need to have?

Whatever you already have. Exports from your point-of-sale system such as Square, Shopify or Clover, Excel sheets, accounting files, even photos of paper logs. If you're unsure, bring it to the consultation and we'll look together.

How much does it cost?

Every project has a fixed price based on how much data you have and what you need. After a free consultation you'll receive a written quote, and nothing begins until you approve it.

Is my business data safe?

Yes. Your data is used only for your project, stored securely and never shared or sold. I'm happy to sign a confidentiality agreement, and I can delete your files at the end of the engagement on request.

Do I need to buy any software?

Usually not. I recommend the simplest option that fits your business, often a tool you already use like Excel. If a paid tool would genuinely help, I'll explain the cost before you decide.

How long does a project take?

Most cleanup and dashboard projects are delivered within one to two weeks. Your proposal will include a specific timeline.
